🌐 Data Sources & Architecture

RainSense Farmer integrates high-resolution meteorological models from Open-Meteo, satellite precipitation estimation (GPM/IMERG compatible), and volumetric soil moisture models (ERA5-Land 0–9 cm depth).

🤖 Machine Learning Engine

Trained on multi-season Indian agro-climatic datasets using Random Forest (Rain Occurrence Classification) and Gradient Boosting (Rainfall Regression) with strictly backward-looking lag features to eliminate data leakage.

⚠️ Field Advisory & Limitations

Satellite rainfall is an estimate. Soil moisture is modeled. Weather conditions can evolve rapidly. This platform is designed as agricultural decision support and should always be paired with physical ground observation.
